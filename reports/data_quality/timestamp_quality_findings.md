# Timestamp Quality Findings

**Source:** `data/raw/olist_orders_dataset.csv`

**Rows checked:** 99,441 (**99,441** distinct order IDs; no records modified or removed)
**KPI alignment:** Checks follow Member 3's current KPI draft: unique-order grain, delivered-status eligibility for delivery KPIs, missing dates excluded and reported separately, elapsed timestamps for durations, calendar dates for lateness, and negative/extreme durations investigated rather than silently excluded. The KPI definitions remain pending team approval; confirm this interpretation with Member 3 before publishing KPIs.

## Duration profile

- Valid delivered purchase-to-actual-delivery durations: **96,470**
- Median: **10.217477 days**
- 95th percentile: **29.274046 days**
- 99th percentile: **46.050262 days**
- Maximum: **209.628611 days**
- Above the exploratory 99th-percentile threshold: **965**

The 99th-percentile cutoff is a diagnostic flag only, not an approved exclusion or winsorization rule. Review those orders with the team; do not alter KPI eligibility without a documented decision.

## Exceptions for review

See `timestamp_quality_exceptions.csv` for order-level records involved in delivered-order missing timestamps, invalid non-empty timestamps, chronology issues, non-delivered status conflicts, and durations above the exploratory 99th percentile. An order may appear more than once when it has multiple issues. The expected-missing non-delivered timestamp context is summarized separately in `timestamp_quality_expected_missing_by_status.csv`.

`order_delivered_carrier_date` is treated as a recorded carrier-related timestamp only. Member 3's KPI draft cautions that its operational meaning as an exact physical carrier handoff still needs confirmation.
